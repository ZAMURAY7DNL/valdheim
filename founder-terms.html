📜 Founder Terms

The full terms for Founder Collection supporters and monthly patrons of VALDHEIM: Clans of the North. The support page shows a short version of these terms next to the collection; this page is the complete text.

📜 Founder Terms.
  • Founder Collection rewards are digital in-game items, delivered as the game launches. Art shown is concept art — the final version is still in development.
  • Founder supporters receive double in-game coins for their donations when Realm 1 launches, and keep their Founder level on the account they registered with.
  • Monthly tiers are real subscriptions (PayPal, auto-charge, cancel anytime). Past months are never refunded; future charges stop on cancellation.
  • A PayPal dispute or chargeback voids your rewards and Founder status.
  • Full refund if the game never launches — see the FAQ below. That promise stands for every supporter, whenever they joined.
🛒 Discount conditions. Discounts apply only on the game's official website, with your registered account. They stack with any offers and discounts launched in that store. The 10% discount replaces the 5% one — they do not add up.
