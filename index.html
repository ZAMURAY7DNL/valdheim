01 · The Game

A strategy game with an identity of its own

Inspired by the great realm-strategy games, with one clear focus: combat where decisions truly matter, and clans you enjoy being part of — not just orders you follow.

⚔️

Tactical combat

Open-field marches, several sides clashing at once, and a troop counter wheel (archers, lancers, infantry) where your choices count.

🛡️

Clans for everyone

A clan system where the leader and every single member have a role, goals and rewards of their own.

🗺️

A shared realm

A living map with players and neutral camps, where conquering, defending and forging alliances have real consequences.

🏰

Building & generals

Nine buildings to grow your city, and generals with identities of their own to lead your armies.

02 · Status

Where we are today

Full transparency: this already works — and this is where your support is needed.

✅ Already built

  • Realm map, 200×200
  • Player vs player battles (PvP)
  • Battles against neutral camps (PvE)
  • Realm chat and clan chat
  • 4 playable generals
  • 4 troop families — 12 troop types
  • 9 city buildings

🚧 What's missing

  • A final art pass (today's art is a strong first version)
  • Music and sound ambience (the realm is silent today)
  • A professional 24/7 server (today it runs on a home PC)
  • Richer events and polished mechanics
  • A deeper world and story
  • Refined combat balance and synergies

So far, the effort has gone into code, the combat system, balance and clans. The look keeps evolving on purpose: first the foundation, then the beauty.
